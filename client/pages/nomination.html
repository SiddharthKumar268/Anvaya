Step-by-Step Wizard

1

Identify Assets Without Nomination

▼

Identify all assets that don't have a registered nominee. Create a detailed list including account numbers, branch details, and approximate values.

2

Obtain Legal Heir Certificate / Succession Certificate

▶

Apply for a Legal Heir Certificate or Succession Certificate depending on your assets.

Where to apply: Tehsildar Office or Civil Court
Timeline: 1 - 6 months
Fees: Varies depending on certificate type
3

Submit to Institutions

▶

Submit the certified copies of the certificate along with claim forms to the respective institutions.

Where to submit: Respective banks or asset management companies
Expected timeline: 15 - 45 days post submission
4

Complete Transfer

▶

Follow up with institutions. Once verified, the assets will be transferred to the legal heirs according to the certificate.
