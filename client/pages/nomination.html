Statutory Claim Step-by-Step Roadmap

1

Identify Non-Nominated Assets & Balance Certificates

▼

Collect latest passbooks, statement of holdings, and obtain written balance certificates from the branch confirming non-nomination status.

2

Obtain Tehsildar Legal Heir Certificate (Varis Dakhila)

▶

Apply at your local Revenue Office (Tehsildar / SDM) or online via your state e-District portal. This establishes all surviving family members without civil court litigation.

Authority: Revenue Department / SDM / Tehsildar
Statutory Timeline: 15 – 30 Days
Standard Fee: ₹50 – ₹200 (Government fee stamp)
3

Execute Family NOC & Indemnity Bond on Stamp Paper

▶

All surviving heirs sign a notarized No-Objection Certificate (NOC) giving consent for one primary heir to collect proceeds, backed by an Indemnity Bond with two guarantors.

Stamp Paper Value: ₹100 or ₹500 non-judicial stamp (state specific)
Guarantors: 2 independent earning individuals with KYC
4

Branch Submission & Disbursal within 15-Day SLA

▶

Submit complete dossier to the branch. Under RBI Customer Service Guidelines (Para 19.3), banks must settle deceased claims within 15 days of receipt of complete documentation.
