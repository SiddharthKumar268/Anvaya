What is UDGAM?

  • UDGAM = Unclaimed Deposits – Gateway to Access inforMation.
  • An RBI initiative launched in 2023 to help public search unclaimed deposits.
  • Allows searching across multiple banks in one place.
  • Funds from accounts inactive for 10+ years are transferred to RBI's DEAF (Depositor Education and Awareness Fund).
  • These deposits can still be claimed by rightful owners or legal heirs/nominees.
