What is RBI UDGAM?

  • UDGAM = Unclaimed Deposits – Gateway to Access inforMation, launched by the Reserve Bank of India.
  • Bank accounts dormant for 10+ continuous years have their balances transferred to RBI's DEAF (Depositor Education and Awareness Fund).
  • Under Section 26A of Banking Regulation Act, funds never expire. Legal heirs retain 100% legal entitlement to claim principal plus accrued interest.
  • All 30 major commercial and foreign banks are currently participating.

Form 26AS / AIS Account Discovery

Every bank in India is legally mandated to report interest income or deduct TDS. You can uncover 100% of forgotten accounts in 5 minutes:

  1. Log into the Income Tax e-Filing Portal using deceased's PAN.
  2. Navigate to e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Form 26AS.
  3. Inspect Part A (TDS on Interest): every bank where the deceased earned interest is listed with branch TAN & name.

Tips & Bank Objection Solver

Under RBI Master Circular DBOD.No.DEAF Cell.BC.101/30.01.002/2013-14, banks cannot reject a DEAF claim solely because the physical passbook is lost. Submission of a certified municipal Death Certificate, claimant KYC (Aadhaar & PAN), and a standard indemnity affidavit is legally sufficient.

For accounts without nomination, RBI guidelines allow banks to settle claims up to the bank's threshold limit (typically ₹1 Lakh to ₹5 Lakhs depending on the bank) upon submission of a joint claim by all legal heirs with a Letter of Indemnity. A formal Succession Certificate is only mandated if the balance exceeds the bank's board-approved ceiling or if there is an active dispute.
